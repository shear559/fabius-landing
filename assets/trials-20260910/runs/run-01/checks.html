Qualitative design review

A condition-blind agent reviewed these actual captured pixels. These observations are not a numerical design score or a general winner claim.

Hierarchy

The desktop hero pairs a compact two-line serif proposition with a framed notebook illustration. The dark workflow action is prominent and the adjacent Peek inside link is clearly secondary.

Text, product preview and next actions are legible without the headline consuming the full opening composition.

Typography and visual coherence

Dark green serif headings, muted green italic emphasis, cream/sage surfaces, thin rules and small source/document motifs recur throughout. The Studio plan uses a dark inverse surface while preserving the same type and spacing language.

The page maintains a recognizable research-notebook identity across the hero, workflow, plans and FAQ.

Product illustration

The hero preview combines a notebook window, note contents and a source card extending outside the frame. The Connect panel links an observation card to a source; the workflow adds small project, source and Markdown illustrations.

The visuals teach the product’s source-and-note relationship instead of functioning only as decoration. Visible demonstration captions appropriately qualify the previews.

Fine-detail readability

Main headings and descriptions are readable, but notebook toolbar labels, tiny preview metadata and captions are substantially smaller. In the mobile notebook preview those details become particularly fine.

The illustration communicates structure and atmosphere more readily than its individual small labels; it should not be treated as a readable product screenshot at every scale.

Mobile first viewport

At the captured mobile width the two-line headline, explanation and both hero actions fit, and a sizeable portion of the notebook preview is visible below them. The header has an icon-only menu control.

The opening reveals the product illustration within the first screen while retaining a clear primary action. No obvious overlap or clipped primary text is visible; this does not certify the rest of the mobile page.

Information states and emphasis

The pricing layout centers two plans with large yearly values and a strong visual emphasis on Studio through a dark card and light action button. The FAQ uses separate rows, a clearly expanded answer and a restrained final callout.

The information is readily scannable. The visual premium-plan emphasis is a presentation choice, not evidence of measured conversion or superior product value.
