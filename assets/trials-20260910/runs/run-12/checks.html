Qualitative design review

A condition-blind agent reviewed these actual captured pixels. These observations are not a numerical design score or a general winner claim.

Hierarchy

The desktop hero gives the large serif headline and the research-notebook illustration distinct columns. The dark primary button sits directly below the supporting explanation, while the navigation is quieter.

The eye can find the proposition, a concrete product representation and the next action without competing card clusters.

Typography and readability

Serif display text, italic emphasis and small sans-serif explanatory text recur across sections. The display hierarchy is clear, but the notebook microcopy, workflow descriptions and FAQ answer use noticeably smaller text than the headings.

The page has an editorial character, while smaller explanatory details require closer reading, especially inside the scaled notebook illustration.

Visual coherence

Ivory surfaces, dark green text, pale green panels, thin dividers and restrained arrow/star marks repeat consistently. The dark workflow band provides a clear change of section without introducing a new palette.

The hero, feature panel, pricing and FAQ appear to belong to one deliberate visual system.

Product illustration

The tilted notebook window includes a project list, a note, an attached source, tags and a separate question card. The Connect panel changes to linked question/note cards.

The illustrations make notes, source context and connections tangible. They remain illustrative interfaces rather than evidence of a working notebook product.

Mobile first viewport

At the captured mobile width the headline, body copy, labelled Menu control and primary action fit the canvas. The notebook illustration begins within the first viewport, although most of its contents continue below it.

The opening communicates the main action promptly and hints at the product; reading the illustrated notebook requires scrolling. No obvious overlap or clipped primary text is visible in this capture.

Pricing and answer presentation

The yearly prices are visually dominant and carry a nearby yearly label. The two plans have distinct but coordinated surfaces. The expanded FAQ uses a clear question/answer separation and leaves the other questions visible.

The captured pricing and answer states remain easy to scan. These observations describe presentation, not a numerical design score or a complete interaction audit.

Review limits

  • Only the supplied captured states were reviewed. Mobile coverage here is the initial viewport, not a complete mobile walkthrough.
  • These static viewport captures do not evaluate motion quality, keyboard behavior, all scroll positions, every responsive breakpoint, or color contrast ratios.
  • Some anchored desktop captures start partway through a preceding heading; that is viewport framing, not evidence that the source page clips that heading.
  • The notebook mockups illustrate a fictional product. Their apparent controls are not evidence of implemented notebook functionality.
  • Both inspected candidates use a coherent serif-and-green editorial direction. These observations do not establish a general winner, causal improvement, or which condition produced either artifact.
