04 / A few good questions

Before you settle in.

A little clarity on what local-first means for your work.

Where do my notes live?

Your notes are stored locally on your device. Lattice is a fictional local-first notebook, designed around keeping your research on the device you use.

Can I take my notes to another app?

Yes. Export your notes as Markdown files, a portable text format you can open in a text editor or bring into another app that supports Markdown.

How do I back up my research?

Export your notes as files and keep copies in a backup location you control. Lattice does not promise automatic cloud backup; regularly exporting files is how you make a backup.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancellation has no effect on files you have already exported. Those files remain with you and can be opened independently of Lattice.
