04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
to know.

Clear answers.
More room for your own questions.

✳
Where do my notes live?+

Your notes are stored locally on your device. Lattice is designed around keeping your research in your own hands.

Can I take my notes to another app?+

Yes. Export your notes as Markdown files, a readable text format that works with many writing and note-taking apps.

How do I back up my research?+

Export your notes as files and keep copies in a backup location you choose, such as an external drive. Lattice does not promise automatic cloud backup; you manage your exported backups.

What happens if I cancel?+

Cancellation has no effect on files you have already exported. Those Markdown files remain yours to open, edit, and keep independently of Lattice.
