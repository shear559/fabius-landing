04 / A little clarity

Good questions.
Straight answers.

The practical things,
before you follow your curiosity.

Where do my notes live?+

Your notes are stored locally on your device. Lattice is a local-first notebook, so your device is the home for your research.

Can I take my notes somewhere else?+

Yes. Export your notes as Markdown files, a readable format you can open in a text editor or use in other Markdown-compatible tools.

How do I back up my work?+

Export your notes as files and keep a separate copy somewhere safe, such as an external drive. Backups are your responsibility; Lattice does not promise automatic cloud backup.

What happens if I cancel?+

Cancellation has no effect on files you have already exported. Those Markdown files stay with you, and you can keep reading and using them independently of Lattice.
