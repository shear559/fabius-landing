04 / THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Your research deserves a little certainty.

Where do my notes live?+

In this product concept, your notes are stored locally on your device. Lattice is designed around keeping your research close to you, with optional full-text search that also runs locally. This demonstration page does not store notes.

Can I take my notes somewhere else?+

Yes. Lattice’s proposed Markdown export gives you readable files you can open in a text editor or bring into another Markdown-compatible tool. Your thinking can move with you.

How would I back up my work?+

Export your notes as files and keep a copy in a separate location you control, such as an external drive. Make exporting a regular habit. Automatic cloud backup is not included or promised.

What happens if I cancel?+

Cancellation has no effect on files you have already exported. Those files remain yours to read, edit, and keep. The prices and plans here are illustrative; this demo has no active subscriptions to cancel.
