      How it was made

      From a script to a file you can keep.

      No camera and no editing timeline. Scenes are web pages; each frame is drawn from its time alone, in a fresh page.

        Contrast, measured on the frames

        Each line is captured at rest, then captured again with only its letters made transparent. The pixels the letters fully cover give the text colour as rendered; the second capture gives the exact pixels behind them. “Thinnest strokes” is the 5th percentile of those pixels; “MP4” repeats the measurement on the decoded H.264 frame.
