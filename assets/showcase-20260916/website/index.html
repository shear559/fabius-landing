A few good questions

Your work.
Your ground rules.

Where do my notes live?+

The Lattice concept keeps notes on your local device. This page is a fictional demonstration, not a released notebook.

Can I take my writing elsewhere?+

Yes. The proposed notebook exports Markdown files that you can open in another editor.

How do I back up my research?+

Export your files and keep a separate backup. Automatic cloud backup is not included or promised.

What happens if I cancel?+

Files you have already exported remain yours. Cancellation does not affect those local files.
