Questions

Frequently asked

Where does my data live?

On your device. Notes are stored locally rather than on a remote server, so your working notebook stays with you.

Can I get my notes out in a usable format?

Yes. Any note or project can be exported to plain Markdown, so the files stay readable and portable outside Lattice.

How do backups work?

Backups happen by exporting your files yourself. There is no automatic cloud backup promised — exporting regularly is how you keep a copy safe.

What happens to my notes if I cancel?

Cancelling has no effect on files you've already exported — those Markdown files are yours and stay exactly as they were.
