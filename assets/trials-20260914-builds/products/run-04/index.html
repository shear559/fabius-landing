Your research stays on your machine.

Lattice is a local-first notebook for independent researchers. Capture notes where you work, keep the original source beside every claim, and export everything to plain Markdown whenever you like — no account, no sync service, no lock-in.

Everything stays close to the work

Three parts of the same notebook: write it down, keep the receipt, take it with you.

Write it down where you already are

Every note is a plain text file on your own disk. Start from a blank page or a reusable project template — literature review, interview log, experiment journal — so a new research thread starts with the structure you already trust.

  • New notes save to your device the moment you stop typing
  • Reusable templates for recurring project shapes
  • Tag and rename freely; nothing is locked behind a server

Keep the source next to the claim

Paste a link while you write and it sits beside the note it supports, not in a separate bookmarks app you'll forget to check. When your notebook grows past memory, an optional local full-text search finds the right page without sending a single keystroke off your device.

  • Source links attach directly to the note that cites them
  • Optional full-text search runs entirely on-device
  • No account needed to link, tag or search

Leave whenever you want

Export any note, or the whole notebook, to standard Markdown files you can open in any editor, commit to your own backup, or hand to a co-author. Exporting is also how you back up: Lattice keeps your files local, so a folder of exported Markdown is your archive.

  • One-click Markdown export, per note or in full
  • Files open in any Markdown-aware editor
  • Exported files are unaffected by anything that happens to your plan later

A three-step workflow

The same shape every time, however deep the research runs.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Open a blank note or a project template and write. It saves to your device as you go, with no sync step in the way.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Drop the source link next to the note it backs up. Search across every note on your device when the notebook grows large.

  3. 03

    Export

    Pull a note or the whole notebook out as Markdown whenever you need it elsewhere — that export is also your backup.

Frequently asked

The parts of the fine print worth reading before the rest.

Where does my data actually live?

On your device, in local storage that Lattice manages for this demonstration. Nothing is uploaded to a server as part of normal use.

Can I get my notes out in a standard format?

Yes — every note, or the whole notebook, exports to plain Markdown files that open in any Markdown-aware editor.

How do I back up my notebook?

By exporting it. Lattice does not promise automatic cloud backup; exporting your files to Markdown, on a schedule you choose, is the backup.

What happens to my notes if I cancel?

Cancellation has no effect on files you already exported — they stay exactly as you exported them, on your device, independent of any plan.
