What can I actually try?+

Search and read the sample notes, add your own observation, create a two-way connection, edit the working brief and export the whole notebook as Markdown.

Where is my writing stored?+

In this page’s memory. Reloading starts a fresh sample. Use Export Markdown to keep your writing; no cloud account or background synchronization is part of this demo.

Is the research real?+

No. The urban-ecology notebook is an authored synthetic example, designed to demonstrate connected thinking. It does not make empirical claims or cite a real field study.
