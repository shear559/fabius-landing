How these were made

A coordinating Codex session used the Fabius design and engineering guidance, with separate builders for the notebook/workspace, visual media, and technical demonstrations. An independent finish review inspected desktop and mobile captures against the owner’s brief and the biology reference. The refinement preserves earlier editions and their original records. It is not a new paired experiment.

Checks and limits

  • Notebook model checks cover immutable capture updates, reciprocal links, search, source-linked draft generation and export.
  • Workspace checks cover populated fixtures, context/deliverable validation, backward-compatible imports, duplicate IDs, dates and form validation.
  • Mathematics: 254 assertions passed using exact fractions, including 182 certificate identities and 72 solver spot checks across six regimes and five joins.
  • Sentinel: 20 fixed cases, 27 malformed-input cases and 768 valid context/action combinations.
  • Relay: 21,977 checked transitions plus the four shipped scenario recipes.
  • Cohort: 12 scheduler checks, including concurrency, dependency gating, partial failure, targeted retry and a mutation control.
  • The browser record below covers actual interactions, all nine gallery tabs, the eight revised walkthroughs, media playback, mobile layout and production-style content policy.

These are checks of specific fixtures and behaviors, not quality scores or a claim of production security. Mobile results are browser emulation on macOS, not a physical phone test.
