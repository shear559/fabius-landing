Frequently asked questions

What happens during the 14-day free trial?

Every Tidepool Plus subscription starts with a 14-day free trial, whether you choose monthly or yearly billing.

Where does Tidepool have forecasts?

312 surf spots and 41 marinas across Portugal and Morocco, with a 7-day forecast refreshed every 30 minutes.

How does the go / wait call work?

Each spot shows tide height, swell period and direction, and wind, alongside a go / wait call based on the limits you save for your own rides.

Does Tidepool work without signal?

Yes. Tidepool works offline for the last forecast you opened.

What devices does Tidepool run on?

iPhone and Apple Watch. There is no Android app yet.
